Why Ethiopia

Why Ethiopia is the world's premier coffee origin.

From the forests of the southwest to the highlands of the south and east, Ethiopia offers roasters what few origins can: history, diversity and flavor.

Where coffee began

Arabica coffee grew wild in Ethiopia's highland forests long before it was farmed anywhere else.

Heirloom diversity

Generations of local heirloom varieties give Ethiopian coffee a range of flavors that is hard to match.

High-altitude highlands

Coffee grows between about 1,100 and 2,300 meters. Cool nights slow the cherry and build sweetness and acidity.

Distinct regional character

Yirgacheffe is floral, Sidamo is bright, Guji is fruity and Harar is winey. One country, many cups.

Smallholder farms

Most coffee is grown by smallholder families, often under shade trees, and picked by hand.

A living coffee culture

Coffee is part of daily life in Ethiopia. The traditional coffee ceremony is how families and neighbors gather.
